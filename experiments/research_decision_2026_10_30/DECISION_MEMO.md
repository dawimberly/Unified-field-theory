## What the completed calculation settles

The saved first-sheet inventories and dispersion/source audits are now complete for the stated five spectra and their matching prescription. None of these regulator prescriptions has thereby become a positive physical UV completion.

The calculations did useful elimination work: they closed missing-pole loopholes, exposed which regulator comparisons were unmatched, and separated source contacts from propagating response. They are not evidence that a healthy microscopic unification is close or that the next familiar consistency identity would establish one.

The latest new derivation, MEASURE_SCALING.md, narrows a remaining loophole conditionally. With a fixed regulator, a regular common saddle and an N-independent finite-component measure, ordinary measure changes are subleading in the collective action and cannot generically erase a simple leading-N nonlocal pole. Source-conversion contacts can nevertheless be leading in the metric covariance. The assumptions have not been proved for constrained CEV. Real local metric polynomials also cannot reverse the sign of the unchanged scalar cut weight.

## Recommended next step

Prioritize the source-matched constrained/composite route as a definition audit, because it directly tests whether the auxiliary calculation addresses the user's original physical target. Keep the stochastic proposal as an explicit comparison branch. Do not silently replace the QFT, state space or regulator while claiming continuity of the same theory.

The next calculation should have the following written input contract before execution:

1. The finite-regulator variables, action and integration domain, including the allowed metric/composite branch.
2. The regulator applied to the reduced nonpolynomial action and products, not merely a replacement cutoff in an old dimensional/PV bubble.
3. The regulated measure and gauge treatment, with its N scaling and any singular Jacobian identified.
4. The source operator and source-only/metric counterterms, distinguishing free stress, total stress and composite geometry.
5. The order of limits and the Lorentzian/state prescription needed for the particular observable.

Then derive the leading connected two-source response. The first useful comparison is its nonlocal kernel/discontinuity and source overlap, retaining local contacts separately. A mismatch at order N or in the leading source map is itself the result to explain. If only ordinary subleading measure terms differ, quantify that claim rather than treating it as a blanket exclusion of the leading auxiliary result.

This contract does not require inventing new inputs immediately. First look for a published definition that already supplies them, and state precisely where it stops. Adopting a genuinely unspecified measure, physical stochastic dynamics or special contour would be a consequential modeling choice requiring human review. Reading and comparing existing proposals does not require that adoption or routine permission.
