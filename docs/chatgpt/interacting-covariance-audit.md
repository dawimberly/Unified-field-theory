## Research record

## Outcome

**DERIVED** The next test separates two requirements: the coordinate symmetry underlying massless gravity, and a background that actually satisfies the field equations. They are not interchangeable. An interacting scalar control preserves the required classical identities, but its vacuum energy shifts when the interaction changes. A flat background is therefore not automatically preserved with the vacuum subtraction held fixed.

**DERIVED** This is an intermediate covariant induced-gravity calculation. It is NOT the complete interacting extension of the composite-graviton theory, an independent demonstration of a composite graviton, or a unification result. The curvature expansion assumes a background geometry. The Einstein kinetic operator used in the symmetry check is supplied explicitly, not discovered from microscopic variables.

## Interacting model and approximation

**DERIVED** The Euclidean matter action has N real components:

**DERIVED** $S_m = \int \sqrt{g} [ (\partial  \phi )^\frac{2}{2} + m0^2 \phi ^\frac{2}{2} + \lambda  (\phi ^2)^2/(4N) ].$

**DERIVED** We work at leading order in the large-N expansion, with a covariant proper-time cutoff $\Lambda =1$ and minimal curvature coupling. The scalar potential is bounded below for all scanned $\lambda \ge 0; m0^{2}>0$ selects the symmetric phase. Positive quartic coupling is a repulsive-interaction control, not an attractive molecular bound-state model. No ordering transition has been simulated.

**DERIVED** Introducing an auxiliary mass-squared variable chi gives the symmetric saddle equation:

**DERIVED** $\chi  = m0^2 + \lambda  I1(\chi )/(16 \pi ^2),$

**DERIVED** where Ij are proper-time integrals: $I_0=\int _1^\infty \exp\left(-\chi s\right) ds/s^{3}, I_1=\int _1^\infty \exp\left(-\chi s\right) ds/s^{2}, I_2=\int _1^\infty \exp\left(-\chi s\right) ds/s$. Independent numerical integration checks their special-function evaluation. The gap equation is solved, rather than assigning a mass by hand. The symmetric scalar effective-potential curvature is $\chi >0$.

**DERIVED** The effective vacuum energy per component, before its additive subtraction, is

**DERIVED** $U(\chi ) = -(\chi -m0^2)^2/(4 \lambda ) - I0(\chi )/(32 \pi ^2).$

**DERIVED** At $\lambda =0$ the auxiliary term is zero and $\chi =m0^{2}$. Chi is an auxiliary saddle variable with its prescribed integration contour; it is not a canonical propagating field whose energy is minimized along the real chi axis. Its saddle curvature must not be called a physical ghost.

**DERIVED** An independent finite-difference calculation verifies the stationary-envelope identity $dU/d\lambda=(\chi -m0^{2})^{2}/(4 \lambda ^{2})$. The leading Euclidean auxiliary response denominator $1+\lambda \int _0^{1} E1[\chi +x(1-x)Q^{2}]dx/(16 \pi ^{2})$ is positive at all tested momenta. That is a limited Euclidean check, not proof of full Lorentzian spectral positivity or absence of every unwanted mode.

## Induced curvature and vacuum terms together

**DERIVED** For a constant saddle, the heat-kernel curvature term gives $B=N I_1(\chi )/(192 \pi ^{2})$, with Lorentzian effective action $B R-\rho$ plus higher terms. No bare Einstein term is included. Eliminating the auxiliary field does not alter this linear-in-curvature coefficient, since the flat-saddle potential derivative vanishes. Its curvature-dependent response does affect higher-order terms, which were not evaluated here.

**DERIVED** Within each fixed-m0 family, choose one additive vacuum constant to cancel the $\lambda =0$ vacuum. Keep that constant fixed for every lambda in that family. Different m0 families each have their own initial subtraction; they are not comparisons at a single common cosmological counterterm.

## What symmetry does and does not establish

**DERIVED** Twenty random symmetric-tensor and momentum examples verify two identities of the supplied linearized Einstein operator: pure coordinate-gauge perturbations lie in its kernel, and its divergence vanishes. An explicit Fierz-Pauli mass term fails the massless gauge test, as expected. This is a check of the mathematical symmetry target, not a calculation of the full interacting quantum graviton self-energy.

**DERIVED** Separately, we expand the volume term $\rho \sqrt{\det (I+h)}$ and independently fit its linear and quadratic coefficients. Residual rho produces both a nonzero tadpole (flat space is not stationary) and an algebraic quadratic term. The quadratic term alone must NOT be interpreted as a physical graviton mass: expansion about a background that fails the field equations does not support that inference. Nonzero vacuum energy is compatible with coordinate symmetry and calls for a curved background. Thus the vacuum obstruction here differs from the detuned massive/ghost spectrum of the original composite model.

## The interaction stress-energy cannot be omitted

**DERIVED** For a three-component test field, fifteen off-shell finite-difference checks verify the classical Euclidean identity $\operatorname{div}(T)_\nu=(\nabla^2\phi-dV/d\phi)\cdot\partial_\nu\phi$. The FULL quartic contribution is included in T. Maximum residual: $4.86\times10^{-10}$. Omitting that contribution while keeping the interaction in the equations of motion fails, with the smallest tested residual 0.001287.

**DERIVED** This does not prove the quantum Ward identities, but it identifies a concrete mistake to avoid in the next calculation: adding an interaction or changing a mass requires the associated stress-energy and quantum vertex corrections. The old free-field kernel with a substituted mass is not the full interacting theory.

## Interpretation

**DERIVED** No automatic solution of the vacuum-energy problem was found. Symmetry can forbid an independent graviton mass in an appropriate self-consistent effective theory; it does not determine the additive vacuum energy. Published calculations of gravitational Ward identities likewise distinguish the symmetry condition from the vacuum/tadpole condition. We did not reproduce their complete two-loop result.

**DERIVED** What remains for the composite proposal is substantial: a full covariant interacting action, consistent regulator and counterterms, self-consistent vacuum, all diagrams at the chosen approximation order, spin-2 and spin-0 spectra, and universal coupling. The calculation here supplies an interacting matter saddle and consistency controls, not those missing composite-graviton calculations.
