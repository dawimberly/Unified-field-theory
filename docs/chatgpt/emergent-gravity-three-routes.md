## Result

**DERIVED** Three partial reproductions were run: a corrected composite-graviton low-energy calculation, an exactly diagonalized small topological quantum lattice, and a two-component condensate spectrum. They demonstrate different meanings of emergence. None establishes a microscopic theory of our universe, predicts measured G, or shows that atomic binding changes G.

**DERIVED** The composite route addresses gravity most directly. The other two supply controls: collective organization and an effective spacetime description are insufficient on their own.

## 1. Composite-graviton calculation

**UNVERIFIED** Source basis: Carone, Erlich and Vaman, arXiv:1610.08521; corrected signs in Carone, Claringbold and Vaman, arXiv:1710.09367; interpretation and consistency discussion in Carone, arXiv:1911.13166. General covariance, constrained total stress-energy, a large number N of scalars, counterterms and a finite covariant regulator are assumptions of this construction. The flat-space matter stress-energy that couples to the emergent mode need not vanish.

**DERIVED** We independently evaluated an off-diagonal transverse-traceless (TT) loop component using Feynman parameters and isotropic dimensional integration. In Euclidean momentum its normalized value is J(Q)=integral_0^1 [1+x(1-x)Q^2/m^2]^(D/2) dx. Its expansion is 1+D Q^2/(12m^2)+D(D-2)Q^4/(240m^4)+higher orders. Numerical quadrature agrees with these coefficients; this is a partial loop calculation, not a reconstruction of every tensor component.

**DERIVED** The supplied corrected kernel obeys A=A0+KA. Its symmetric-index form was represented as a 16 by 16 matrix and solved directly. The solution agrees with the published leading massless pole when lambda=-1. Independent on-shell scalar external factors satisfy momentum conservation, allowing the longitudinal terms to drop from that scattering calculation. The pole residue agrees with the separately supplied Planck-coefficient expression. This is a reproduction of known formulas, not a new derivation of universal gravity.

**DERIVED** At N=100, m=1 and finite regulator D=4.02 (epsilon=-0.02), Q/m=0.01 gives a normalized Euclidean TT pole residue 0.9999899001; it approaches one as Q approaches zero. D here is a dimensional-regularization parameter, not a model with 4.02 physical spacetime dimensions and not our earlier 8-12 dimensional scan.

### Change the ingredients, holding the interaction fixed

**DERIVED** Changing m at fixed N, V0 and regulator shifts lambda away from -1. The published low-momentum denominator gives the pole estimate m_g^2=12m^2(1+lambda)/(D lambda). A +0.1 percent constituent mass change gives m_g^2=+0.0119939 in reference m=1 units; a -0.1 percent change gives -0.0120061. Larger scanned changes are retained in results.json but the leading derivative expansion is not trustworthy once the inferred pole approaches the constituent scale. A conservative |m_g^2|/m^2<0.05 plotting gate is a numerical choice, not an observational bound.

**DERIVED** Negative squared pole mass signals a flat-background instability. A positive squared mass is also not sufficient: in the original detuned construction the unchanged massless numerator decomposes into a healthy massive spin-2 exchange plus a scalar with negative residue. In four dimensions, [T.T'-(1/2)TT']=[T.T'-(1/3)TT']-(1/6)TT'. We verified this decomposition. It is a ghost warning, not a healthy screened-gravity model. The 2019 review proposes a reformulation without clock-and-ruler fields that avoids this particular limiting construction; its complete quantum consistency was not tested here.

### Retune after changing the ingredients

**DERIVED** With regulator fixed and the massless condition reimposed, M_P^(D-2) is proportional to N m^(D-2). Doubling N halves the effective coupling in this comparison. Doubling m multiplies it by 0.246558 at D=4.02, while V0 must increase by 16.22335. These compare different parameter choices, not a binding reaction inside one Hamiltonian. We have not shown that a physical ordering transition performs the required retuning.

**DERIVED** Not executed: the full six-external-scalar loop calculation of the three-graviton vertex, additional diagrams from a new quartic scalar interaction, an interacting phase transition, the full finite-N theory, or a UV completion. The published self-interaction result is a literature result, not a numerical result of this script. Simply changing m in the free-field kernel does not calculate an interacting bound state.

## 2. Collective particles on a quantum lattice

**DERIVED** An eight-spin, 256-dimensional square-torus Hamiltonian was exactly diagonalized. This is Kitaev's Z2 toric code, a simple topological-order control, not a replication of every Levin-Wen string-net model. We used H=-Je sum A_vertex-Jm sum B_face-h sum X-0.37h sum Z.

**DERIVED** At Je=Jm=1,h=0 the ground energy is -8, its degeneracy is four, and the first excited energy is -4. An edge operator creates two defects costing four energy units; applying it again annihilates the pair. Crossing noncontractible loop operators anticommute while both commute with the unperturbed Hamiltonian. These identify collective degrees of freedom absent from a description of isolated spins.

**DERIVED** Thirty parameter combinations vary vertex strength, plaquette strength and applied fields. The ground-state splitting and order diagnostics change. For Je=Jm=1, h=0.2 the lowest splitting is 0.0903067; at h=1 it is 2.75434. This tiny system cannot establish a bulk phase transition, thermodynamic robustness, particle statistics by an explicit braiding process, or an infrared continuum gravitational theory.

**DERIVED** A separate three-component Maxwell quadratic control has two transverse modes and a longitudinal gauge direction. Its vector transformation law is spin 1, illustrating why merely counting two propagating modes does not identify a spin-2 graviton. Gauge structure was assumed in this control. Neither lattice calculation derives Einstein dynamics. Levin-Wen string-net work demonstrates emergent gauge/fermionic behavior in appropriate models; that broader result is background literature, not a new result of our eight-spin run.

## 3. Collective waves and effective geometry

**DERIVED** For an equal-density two-component condensate, the full four by four Bogoliubov matrix was diagonalized for 36 coupling/wave-number combinations, including both signs of intercomponent coupling. Units are hbar=m=n=g=1. Independent analytic branches are omega_plus/minus^2=epsilon_k[epsilon_k+2(1+/-g12)], with epsilon_k=k^2/2. The matrix eigenvalues match.

**DERIVED** At g12=0 the low-momentum speeds agree. At |g12|=0.5 the speeds are sqrt(0.5) and sqrt(1.5); one slows while the other speeds up. At |g12|=0.9 they are sqrt(0.1) and sqrt(1.9). At |g12|>1 one long-wavelength branch is unstable. At |g12|=1 one branch has quadratic rather than linear dispersion. Changing the sign of g12 exchanges which collective combination softens. It does not make every fluctuation calm down together.

**DERIVED** Separately, four acoustic-background examples verify sqrt(-det g) g_inverse against the linearized fluid wave operator, including subsonic, sonic and supersonic values. These are local algebraic consistency checks, not globally solved stationary flows, a Hawking-radiation simulation, or a gravitational black hole. The analogy gives curved-spacetime propagation for a scalar disturbance. It supplies neither a massless spin-2 mode nor Einstein's field equation. Different condensate branches generally have different propagation cones, so universal geometry is not automatic.

## Assessment and next discriminating step

**DERIVED** The molecule analogy has a precise foothold: interactions can produce collective degrees of freedom. What remains missing is a mechanism that selects a healthy, universal, massless spin-2 mode and preserves it when the microscopic state changes. The composite model's detuning failure is useful evidence against treating any field combination as automatically gravitational.

**DERIVED** The most informative next step is a symmetry-preserving interacting extension, analyzed around a self-consistent background: include the new scalar interaction and all diagrams of the relevant order; check vacuum stability, the spin-2 pole, unwanted scalar poles and universal coupling together. A full calculation may require analytic field-theory work beyond these numerical checks. A stable soliton, an entangled state, a changed wave speed or a fitted inverse-square force alone would not answer this question.
