## Lower-loop counterterm insertions

**DERIVED** Normalize the constant wavefunction by a covariant field rescaling. Its field-independent Jacobian gives no mass-dependent R term in dimensional regularization. At fixed $a_X=a_Y=0$, the source subtraction is homogeneous in the stated scheme. Thus the lower-loop matter insertions into the baseline are generated by replacing z and h in the bare lower-loop expressions. Use $\beta _z/z=h-h^{2}$ and $\beta _h=-2\epsilon h+3h^{2}+O(h^{3})$ to solve the bare-mass pole equation. The ratios, with the engineering $\mu$ factor suppressed for h, are

**DERIVED** $z_B/z = 1+h/(2\epsilon )+h^{2}[1/(2\epsilon ^{2})-1/(4\epsilon )]$,
**DERIVED** $h_B/h = 1+3h/(2\epsilon )+O(h^{2})$.

**DERIVED** The one-loop bare coefficient is $z e^{-\epsilon L} e^{\gamma \epsilon } \Gamma (\epsilon )/[6K(1-\epsilon )]$, including the spin multiplicity. Its first mass insertion gives $hz/K$ times $[1/\epsilon ^{2}-L/\epsilon ]/12$, as independently fixed by the $w=2$ runnable two-loop benchmark. At order h² the replacement multiplies its logarithm-free prefactor after cancelling $1-\epsilon$ by $[1/(2\epsilon ^{2})-3/(8\epsilon )]$.

**DERIVED** The bare two-loop coefficient is $|y|^2(1-2\epsilon)I_{\mathrm{bare}}/36$. Replacing h and z multiplies it by $h(2/\epsilon -1)$ at the requested order. For its finite input write $I_{bare} K^{2}/z = -3/(2\epsilon ^{2})+(3L-9/2)/\epsilon -3L^{2}+9L+C-3-3\zeta (2)/2$, with $C=-15/2+2\sqrt3\operatorname{Cl}_2(\pi/3)$. This follows from the subtracted sunset value in the two-loop benchmark and adding the order$-\epsilon$ terms of three equal-mass bare tadpoles divided by $\epsilon$. There is no new three-loop integral in this matter-counterterm step.

## What remains for the four-Yukawa graphs

**DERIVED** The [latest completed bare subtotal through CcFf](ffz-ccff-regulated-poles.md) is $5/(144\epsilon^2)+[-0.1506-5L/48]/\epsilon$ (simple constant approximate). There is no triple pole in that subtotal. Subtract it and the counterterms from the Y coefficients $(1/36, -1/18, 1/24)$. The conditions on FFff$(A+B)$ for a projected cancellation are

- **DERIVED** Triple pole $1/36$.
- **DERIVED** Double pole $1/72-L/12$.
- **DERIVED** Simple pole at $L=0$ approximately 0.1071. The exact target is $1/24$ minus the bare subtotal's simple constant and minus $C/18+3/8-\zeta (2)/24$.
- **DERIVED** Simple-pole logarithms $+L^{2}/8-L/24$. They follow from multiplying the bare constant triple and double poles by $\exp\left(-3\epsilon L\right)$. Adding the subtotal and counterterm logarithms cancels both $L^{2}/\epsilon$ and $L/\epsilon$.

**UNVERIFIED** The [localized FFff(B) triple pole](ffz-ffff-b-mixed-endpoints.md) gives $1/48$, so A and the unverified remainder of B must together supply $1/144$ to meet the triple-pole check. This is a constraint on the remaining integrals. The simple-pole target is a test, with its displayed precision limited by the existing bare subtotal.

from fractions import Fraction as F
from math import pi

for L in (F(0), F(2,5), F(-1)):
    one3 = F(1,12)
    one2 = -L/12-F(1,16)
    two3 = 2*F(-3,2)/36
    two2 = (2*(3*L-F(3,2))+F(3,2))/36
    assert one3+two3 == 0
    assert one2+two2 == L/12-F(5,48)
    target3 = F(1,36)
    target2 = -F(1,18)-F(5,144)-one2-two2
    assert target2 == F(1,72)-3*L*target3
    assert F(9,2)*L*L*target3-3*L*F(1,72)-5*L/48-L*L/8+7*L/48 == 0
assert F(1,36)-F(1,48) == F(1,144)
C = 1.343907238689459*1.5-6
ct_simple = C/18+3/8-pi*pi/144
print("CT simple at L=0:", ct_simple)
print("Remaining Yukawa simple target at L=0:", 1/24+0.1506-ct_simple)
