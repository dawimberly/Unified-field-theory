## Research record

## Outcome and scope

**DERIVED** An exact four-site spin-1/2 calculation supplies a controlled pair of states with the same Hamiltonian, spin-sector energy and vanishing mean total spin, but different spin correlations. It does not calculate gravity, establish a spin-dependent Newton constant, or show that quantum spin has been misinterpreted. Intrinsic spin is distinct from classical rotation; spin alignment, quantum correlations, electrical polarization and orbital angular momentum are different state variables.

**DERIVED** The model is H=-J sum_nearest S_i dot S_j on a periodic four-site ring, with hbar=1. The sites represent distinguishable localized spin degrees of freedom; orbital wavefunctions, Maxwell fields, material elastic modes, temperatures and the apparatus are omitted. The compared states are energy eigenstates, not necessarily equilibrium phases or a claimed laboratory preparation protocol.

## What the control permits

**DERIVED** A model coupling only to mean spin would give no response for both zero-mean states. A model sensitive to two-spin correlations could distinguish them. This is a construction of an observable distinction, not a derived gravitational coupling. A finite ring's total-spin-squared operator is not automatically a local continuum source. Locality, orbital overlap and coarse graining must be supplied before identifying it with any field-theory operator.

## Academic routes and model boundaries

1. **DERIVED** CEV scalar fields have intrinsic spin zero. Spin-2 labels the emergent gravitational response, not the constituent particle spin. Existing scalar results do not constitute an intrinsic-spin test. A fermionic constituent action, its regulator and contact terms must be specified to investigate this direction.
2. **DERIVED** Einstein-Cartan-type theories already study spin as a source of torsion. In minimal Einstein-Cartan gravity torsion is algebraic; eliminating it gives local fermion interactions rather than a generic new long-range torsion force. More general torsion dynamics are additional assumptions, not a consequence of using the word spin. A torsion balance instrument uses a twisting suspension and does not by that name demonstrate spacetime torsion.
3. **DERIVED** Zero mean axial spin current does not generally imply zero axial-current correlations. A calculation must distinguish the renormalized expectation of a product from the product of expectation values. This matters for microscopic fermion models and their macroscopic averages.

## Proposed field-theory extension, not an established interaction

**DERIVED** Let psi be a Dirac constituent and J5^mu=bar(psi) gamma^mu gamma5 psi. Begin with a covariant kinetic term plus a specified interaction capable of preparing correlated states. In an effective-theory operator inventory one may examine

  Delta L = zeta R [J5_mu J5^mu]_ren / Lambda^4
           + eta R_mu_nu [J5^mu J5^nu]_ren / Lambda^4.

**DERIVED** Both operators have the correct four-dimensional mass dimension. Their coefficients, signs and presence are assumptions until matched to microscopic physics. They are not derived by the spin-chain calculation. Operator redundancies, field redefinitions, renormalization and matter contacts have to be treated before calling either term a change of Newton's constant. In particular, moving a curvature-matter term into other operators cannot change a physical scattering amplitude.

**DERIVED** A polarized medium also supplies a preferred rest frame and direction. Its response is generally anisotropic and frequency dependent; there need not be a single material-dependent number G. Merely assigning a field-dependent coefficient to R would assume part of the effect under investigation.

## Concrete response test

**DERIVED** For the specified fermion theory, compute the retarded stress response in each prepared state:

  K_mn,rs(omega,k) = local metric contacts + connected retarded stress-stress response.

**DERIVED** Resolve its metric constraints and scalar/vector/tensor mixing. Compare the resulting conserved-source exchange amplitudes between states, accounting for energy, stresses and electromagnetic field changes. In a medium, separate static response from propagating-wave response. Test all poles, residues and causal prescriptions rather than only the coefficient of one tensor component.

**DERIVED** An apparent difference should be assigned to an ordinary energy/stress effect, additional force, anisotropic medium response, or a changed long-range spin-2 coupling only after that observable calculation. The current numerical control provides the spin-correlation distinction but none of those gravitational verdicts.
