# Planck→GUT stress test for U survivors

Runnable: `ffz_planck_stress.py` + test. Stacked on #48.  
**No tuning.** \(\Lambda_{\mathrm{UV}}=M_{\mathrm{Pl}}\), \(M_{\mathrm{GUT}}=10^{16}\,\mathrm{GeV}\).

## Results (central)

| Mechanism | \(S\) | Flip? | Key assumption |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Baseline SM | \(\sim-316\) | no | free-field thresholds |
| Complete Higgsing (short Planck→GUT only) | \(\sim-117\) | **no** | SM restored below \(M_{\mathrm{GUT}}\) |
| Complete Higgsing \(M_{\mathrm{Pl}}\to\mathrm{EW}\) | **+** | **yes** | no massless γ/g until EW (**IR conflict**) |
| SO(10) heavy Proca (no Higgs) | \(\sim+373\) | **yes** | break at \(M_{\mathrm{GUT}}\) |
| E6 heavy Proca (3×27) | \(\sim+1306\) | **yes** | 3×27 **assumed** |
| KK level-1 extras (short) | \(\sim-231\) | **no** | 1 KK level **assumed** |
| Technicolor \(n=4\) (short) | \(\sim-250\) | **no** | 4 Proca above \(M_{\mathrm{GUT}}\) |

## Lesson

Short high-scale Proca windows lose to the long SM bin. GUT chains flip because the **long** broken-to-SM bin carries many Proca. Full Proca to EW flips but fights IR massless gauge bosons.

Coincidence: \(M_{\mathrm{GUT}}\sim10^{16}\), \(\Lambda_{\mathrm{UV}}\sim M_{\mathrm{Pl}}\) — structural.
